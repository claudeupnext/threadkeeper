How to find an old Perplexity answer

You know Perplexity told you something useful a few weeks ago, but the thread title gives no clue. Here is how to get it back.

1. Search your Perplexity library

Your threads live at perplexity.ai/library. Use the search box there and try the words you would have typed in your question, not the answer.

2. Search inside every answer with Threadkeeper

Threadkeeper keeps the full text of every thread you open and searches titles, tags, questions and answers. A word that only appeared deep in an answer still finds the thread.

  1. Install Threadkeeper (free).
  2. Open the threads you care about once so they are saved (new ones are saved as you go).
  3. Click the Threadkeeper icon and type in the search box.
Tip: Threadkeeper can only search threads it has saved. Install it now, and every thread you open from today on becomes searchable.
