How to back up your Perplexity threads

Threads can be deleted, accounts can change, and research you did months ago is easy to lose. A local copy takes a minute.

Option 1: export the threads that matter

Use Perplexity's export on single answers, or Threadkeeper's free whole-thread export to Markdown or PDF. See how to export a Perplexity thread.

Option 2: back up your whole library

  1. Install Threadkeeper (free). Threads you open are saved locally.
  2. Open the library, go to Settings, and click Download backup (.json).
  3. Keep the file somewhere safe. Restore backup brings everything back, on this or another computer.

Want readable files instead of one JSON file? Pro exports the whole library as a ZIP of Markdown files or one PDF.

FAQ

Is the backup private?

Yes. The backup file is created in your browser and saved where you choose. It never leaves your computer unless you move it.
